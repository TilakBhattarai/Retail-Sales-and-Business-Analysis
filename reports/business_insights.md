## 8. Recommendations
1. Cap discounts and require approval for high discount bands.
2. Review pricing and supplier cost for loss-making sub-categories and products.
3. Plan stock and marketing around peak months.
4. Investigate the weakest states before expanding.
5. Offer loyalty benefits to top customers and the best segment.

## 9. Limitations
- Future-dated orders and ship dates were kept after investigation, so some date-based results may include them.
- The analysis is descriptive. It shows what happened, not exactly why.
- Negative-profit rows and outliers were retained in the totals.
